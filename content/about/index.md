## Bor

Most of my free time goes into [Bor](https://getbor.dev/), an open-source policy management platform for Linux desktops. It lets you define, distribute and enforce desktop configuration (browser policies, KDE and GNOME settings, polkit rules, packages) across a whole fleet of machines, in real time. Think Group Policy, but for Linux.

I've been building it at night, on weekends and on holidays since the beginning of 2025, but the idea has been growing in my head for more than 15 years. Looking after Linux computer labs has followed me since the very start of my IT career. It began at [my high school](https://tcom-sf.org/), where I was already maintaining the Linux desktops while still a student there. Later came [ESFAM](https://esfam.auf.org/), formerly IFAG, and the [Francophone Centre at Sofia University](https://www.uni-sofia.bg/index.php/bul/universitet_t/centrove/frankofonski_cent_r), whose Ubuntu workstations became the subject of [my very first blog post](/post/2013-10-04-ubuntu-12-04-desktop-with-zentyal-3-2/). In recent years I've been helping the [ZaSelata foundation](http://zaselata.org/) build Linux computer labs in village community centres, the Bulgarian читалища ([more about that](/post/2020-10-08-revival-of_the-bulgarian-villages/)).

Every one of those places needed the same thing: a way to keep many Linux desktops configured and under control without walking from machine to machine. Bor is the tool I wished I had in all of them.
